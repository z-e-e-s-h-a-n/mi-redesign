      FAQ

      Questions practices ask us first.

      How much does it cost?

      Most practices are billed a percentage of collections, so our incentive matches yours — we only grow when your collections grow. Pricing depends on specialty, claim volume and which parts of the cycle you hand over. There are no setup fees and no long-term lock-in.

      Do we have to change our EHR or practice management system?

      No. We work inside the system you already use. Our team is trained on the major EHR/PM platforms and we adapt to your workflow rather than forcing a migration.

      How is patient data protected?

      Every workflow is HIPAA-compliant. Access is role-based and least-privilege, activity is logged, transmissions are encrypted, and staff complete recurring HIPAA training. A BAA is signed before any PHI is exchanged.

      What happens to our existing aged A/R?

      We work it. Old A/R clean-up runs as a parallel project alongside new claims, starting with the highest-value recoverable balances and anything approaching a timely-filing deadline.

      How fast will we see a difference?

      Clean-claim improvements show up in the first submission cycle. Most practices see a measurable drop in denials within 30–60 days and a meaningful reduction in days in A/R within one quarter.

      Do you handle credentialing too?

      Yes — commercial payers, Medicare and Medicaid, CAQH build and attestation, revalidations and re-credentialing reminders. Getting in-network faster is often the single biggest revenue unlock for a new provider.
